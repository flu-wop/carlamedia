About Carla

A Buffalo girl who fell for New Orleans food

I've been making videos since I was a kid. I turned that into a career in TV news, and then into CarlaMedia — a media company that tells the stories behind the restaurants and food brands of Southeast Louisiana.

At every station I worked, I built a weekly restaurant segment spotlighting a local hidden gem. I competed on MasterChef Season 13 on Fox as the first Buffalo native in the MasterChef kitchen, and today I judge and emcee food festivals across the region.

Off camera you'll find me fishing, cooking, playing beach volleyball, or at the park with my dog, Teddy.
